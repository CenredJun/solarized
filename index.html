Choose the Right Solar System

Three system types — one perfect fit for your needs.

On-Grid (Grid-Tied)

  • Connected to utility grid
  • Lowest cost option
  • Ideal for homes with reliable grid
  • Net metering: sell excess back
  • No battery needed

Hybrid (Best of Both)

  • Grid-connected + battery backup
  • Power during outages
  • Maximize self-consumption
  • Net-meter excess production
  • Most popular choice

Off-Grid (Standalone)

  • Fully independent from grid
  • Perfect for remote locations
  • Requires battery storage
  • Complete energy independence
  • Best for cabins & islands
